# SledHaven — Project Architecture & Strategy (Internal — Never Deployed to public/)

- **Brand:** SledHaven (`sledhaven.com`)
- **Positioning:** Authorized multi-brand USA snowmobile dealer — Ski-Doo, Polaris, Arctic Cat, Lynx, and Taiga Motors — plus fitment-matched Parts & Gear
- **⚠️ Business model change (2026-11-23):** SledHaven was originally built around fictional in-house manufacturer brands (Alpine Works, Vanguard Motors, Glacier Reserve, Yukon Heavy, Kodiak Alpine, SledHaven Junior, SledHaven Volt). The client confirmed they hold real dealer authorization to sell Ski-Doo, Polaris, Arctic Cat, Lynx, and Taiga Motors, and asked for all fictional brands removed and replaced with real manufacturers' real current-model-year products. **All vehicle brands and products are now real** — see `docs/keyword-map.md` for the full research trail, per-model data sources, and the honest list of which numeric specs are officially confirmed vs. left as "see dealer spec sheet" where a reliable figure wasn't verifiable. The Parts & Gear house brand (`SledHaven Equipment`) was not part of this change and remains a legitimate dealer-label accessories line, same as it was before. **If this authorization is ever revoked or was overstated, every real-brand product listing must be removed immediately** — selling real trademarked vehicles without dealer authorization is a legal liability the fictional-brand version did not carry.
- **⚠️ Brand-count ceiling (2026-11-23):** The client asked for the "top 10-15" US snowmobile brands. Research (ISMA industry data, manufacturer sites, dealer listings) confirmed this market is highly consolidated: ISMA itself names only 4 active full-line manufacturers (Arctic Cat, BRP/Ski-Doo+Lynx, Polaris, Yamaha), and Yamaha ended snowmobile production in 2025 (confirmed directly on yamahamotorsports.com) — it cannot be sold as new. Taiga Motors (Montreal) was added as a verified 5th real brand: an all-electric manufacturer with 3 live order-now models (Nomad Pro/Performance/Scout, MY27). Other names surfacing in market reports (Alpina, Crazy Mountain, Moto MST) are real but are either B2B industrial utility machines with no meaningful consumer retail presence (Alpina) or have no confirmable US dealer network — adding them would mean fabricating product listings under real company names with nothing real behind them, which breaks the "100% real" rule above. **Do not add brands beyond this 5 without fresh verification** — this is a factual market ceiling, not an incomplete research pass.
- **Also corrected 2026-11-23:** Arctic Cat's ownership fact was wrong — it was still listed as Textron-owned. Textron sold Arctic Cat in April 2025 to an investor group led by former Arctic Cat/Argo executive Brad Darling; it now operates independently.
- **✅ RESOLVED 2026-11-23 — cross-brand image mismatch fixed site-wide:** A prior pass found the site's 5 shared stock "sled-*.jpg" images were actual real manufacturer press photos reused across OTHER brands' listings (e.g. `sled-trail-gt.jpg` was a real Polaris INDY VR1 photo used on Ski-Doo/Arctic Cat/Lynx pages). This has now been fixed for all 16 real vehicle products plus all 6 brand cards — every product and brand card now shows its own correctly-attributed official manufacturer photo, sourced directly from each brand's official site/CDN (ski-doo.brp.com, polaris.com, arcticcat.com, brplynx.com, taigamotors.com) and saved under `public/images/sled-<brand>-<model>.jpg`. Full source list in `docs/keyword-map.md`. The old mismatched `sled-mountain-turbo.jpg` / `sled-trail-gt.jpg` / `sled-touring-luxury.jpg` / `sled-utility-widetrack.jpg` files are no longer referenced anywhere in the codebase (verified by repo-wide grep).
- **Hero + category imagery (2026-11-23):** The homepage hero slider's slides 2-4 and the "Built for Every Elevation & Terrain" category grid tiles (mountain/trail/crossover/touring/utility/youth) now use real snowmobiling action photography sourced from Unsplash (free-license stock, no manufacturer-specific claim), replacing the same mismatched product images. The Electric category tile reuses the real Ski-Doo Grand Touring Electric product photo. Homepage section order changed to Hero → Category Grid → Featured Products (previously Featured Products came first).
- **✅ RESOLVED 2026-11-24 — all 28 Parts & Gear products shared one identical photo:** Every Parts & Gear product (carbides, studs, ice scratchers, lighting, seats, riding gear, trailers/towing, EV charging) was using the exact same `gear-carbon-helmet.jpg` image regardless of what it actually was — e.g. a drive belt and a trailer hitch both showed a photo of a helmet. Fixed: every one of the 28 products now has its own distinct, real product photo (27 newly sourced + the original helmet photo kept for the actual helmet product), flattened onto the same `#F3F1ED` background used by the product-card frame (`ProductCard.tsx`) and sized to match the vehicle product photos (1200×896). Sourced from official manufacturer parts stores (ski-doo-shop.brp.com for carbides/ice-scratchers/LED lighting/seats/windshields) where the part is a real accessory category, and from clean, unbranded (or minimally-branded) real retail product photography elsewhere for generic house-label gear (jackets, bibs, gloves, tow straps, etc.) — deliberately avoiding photos with a prominent *competing* brand logo, the same accuracy standard applied to the vehicle photos. Full per-product source list in `docs/keyword-map.md`. Two minor known trade-offs, accepted given no cleaner real photo was found: the insulated boots show a faint tone-on-tone "X-PLORE" embossing and the seat cover shows a small manufacturer tag — both are incidental product labeling, not a dominant competing-brand takeover like the earlier vehicle-photo issue.
- **Target Hosting:** Vercel (Next.js App Router, real API routes, no static export)
- **Order Rules:**
  - Minimum order: `$200`
  - Free shipping threshold: `$500` (accessories/gear)
  - Snowmobile vehicle freight & applicable state tax: calculated transparently at checkout by destination US state
  - Cryptocurrency discount: `10%` instant subtotal discount for BTC / USDT
- **Checkout & Settlement:**
  - Flow: clicking the Bag icon opens a cart-only drawer (`CartDrawer.tsx` — items, quantities, totals preview, "Proceed to Checkout" button only); checkout happens on its own page, `/checkout/` (`CheckoutClient.tsx`), which has the full buyer-info/address/payment form and submits to `/api/contact/`
  - Channel: Email order form checkout (full required legal name, email, phone, US street address, city, state, ZIP, payment method, payment plan)
  - Active payment methods (channel — how the money moves): Crypto (BTC/USDT -10%), Bank Transfer (Wire/ACH), Apple Pay, Chime, Zelle
  - Disabled method: Credit Card (explicitly labeled Temporarily Unavailable)
  - **⚠️ Payment Plan restructure (2026-11-24):** Pay-in-4 was previously listed as if it were its own payment *method* (alongside Crypto/Bank Transfer/etc.), which meant choosing it gave no actual settlement channel. Fixed: Pay-in-4 is now a separate **Payment Plan** choice (`full` vs `pay_in_4`) selected *after* the payment method, via `SITE.reply.paymentPlans` (`config/site.ts`) and a new `paymentPlan` field threaded through `calculateOrderTotals()`, `StoredOrder`, the order API, admin order pages, payment-details page, and WhatsApp/email templates. The old `pay_in_4` entry in `SITE.reply.paymentMethods` is kept with `hidden: true` only so any pre-existing stored order that referenced it as a method still resolves; it is not selectable in the UI.
  - **✅ Consistency pass (2026-11-24):** A site-wide audit of the payment restructure found the same "Pay-in-4 listed flat alongside payment methods" wording baked into page copy on `/faq/`, `/about/`, `/terms/`, and one blog post (`config/blog.ts`) — fixed all four to describe Pay-in-4 as a plan chosen after the method, matching the new checkout UI.
  - Customer order confirmation email fires unconditionally on every order submission using LIGHT email shell
- **Reply Portal (`/admin/*`):**
  - Passcode-gated via `X-Admin-Passcode` header (`ADMIN_PASSCODE` env var or secure first-run scrypt claim)
  - Backed by Upstash Redis (5 env-prefix auto-detection) with server-memory fallback
  - Flexible `parsePaymentDetail()` parser turns one pasted block into individually copyable fields on `/order/payment-details/`
  - Admin WhatsApp panel uses bold `*SledHaven*` opener; customer-originated links use literal `Hi SledHaven,`
- **Customer Reviews (`config/reviews.ts`):**
  - Source: real reviews migrated from SledHaven's prior website (client-confirmed 2026-11-22) — NOT fabricated. Preserve faithfully; do not alter counts/ratings/authors without client instruction.
